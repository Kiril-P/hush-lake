## Diagnosis
- The flat lavender sky occupies much of the frame without a useful gradient or depth.
- The trees and mountains have similar contrast, flattening the distant silhouette.
- The hut's blown-out doorway dominates the camp while nearby stalls are almost black.
- The broad blue foreground ice is uniform, with a harsh white shoreline and few surface cues.
- The Eye's pupil is mostly cropped and its bright streaks compete with the camp.

## Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Darken and desaturate the ice toward teal; add subtle polygonal colour variation and thin cracks, preserving contrast around the aqua Eye and its black pupil.
2. Add blue distance fog between forest and mountains, with a gentle sky gradient to separate their silhouettes.
3. Reduce doorway bloom and place small amber lantern lights on the hut and stalls, with restrained pools of light on adjacent ice.
4. Give the ice edge thickness and soften its white rim; scatter a few faceted slabs near the Eye.
5. Add simple crates, stacked logs and a sled beside existing buildings for scale and lived-in detail.

The painterly cloud texture, intricate reflections and volumetric shafts are illustrative targets; approximate them with simple gradients, decals and limited light effects. The aurora and dusk in the variants depart from clear midday.
