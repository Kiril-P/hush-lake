## Diagnosis
- Pale ice has little tonal variation, flattening the scene.
- Bright white hex borders compete with the Eye.
- The Eye's bright upper and lower bands obscure its radial structure.
- The huts and stalls blend into their circular snow pads and lack readable shadows.
- Scattered fish silhouettes are similarly small and low contrast, creating visual noise.

## Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Darken the central water to deep teal and grade the surrounding ice cooler, preserving a black pupil and aqua iris.
2. Reduce hex-border brightness and width; reserve the thin amber emissive ring for the Eye.
3. Add soft directional shadows and an amber light pool around the hut windows to anchor the structures.
4. Add broad flat ice facets and sparse frost marks, keeping detail quieter near the Eye.
5. Add subtle cool light shafts or a low-opacity atmospheric overlay, strongest away from the pupil.

The storybook variant's intricate painted frost and organic ice textures are illustrative; approximate them with sparse decals or simple shaders rather than expecting primitive geometry to reproduce every mark. The Nordic shafts are a composited lighting impression and may require a shader or volumetric effect.
