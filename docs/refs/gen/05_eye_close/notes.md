## Diagnosis
- The pale sky and forest have little atmospheric separation.
- The Eye's white bloom washes out its aqua surface and rim.
- The foreground ice is a broad uniform blue plane with few depth cues.
- Oversized, uniformly bright shards compete with the Eye's silhouette.
- The distant stalls and forest form a repetitive, flat strip with little warm light.

## Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Reduce Eye bloom; use a dark pupil, textured aqua annulus and thin amber rim.
2. Grade ice toward deep teal and add broad flat facets and subtle cracks.
3. Add distance-based blue fog to separate stalls from layered conifer silhouettes.
4. Place small amber lanterns at stalls and cast localized light onto nearby snow.
5. Vary shard size and spacing, keeping the Eye's outline unobstructed.

Painterly reflections and complex atmospheric shafts are artistic targets; approximate them with simple shaders and lights rather than expecting exact real-time reproduction.
