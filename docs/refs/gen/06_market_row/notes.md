# Diagnosis
- The pale, nearly uniform sky leaves the upper half visually empty.
- Distant trees and mountains have similar contrast, flattening the horizon.
- Stall lanterns read as yellow squares without illuminating nearby surfaces.
- The glowing foreground ice is overexposed, obscuring the Eye's focal shape.
- Repeated bare stall silhouettes and sparse props leave the market feeling unfinished.

# Wins, ordered by impact
1. Reduce foreground bloom and define the Eye with an aqua edge and dark pupil.
2. Add amber point lights at stall corners, with local pools on snow and warm roof-edge highlights.
3. Layer blue-teal distance fog over the forest and mountains to separate silhouettes.
4. Add snow caps, simple crates, barrels and hanging goods around existing stalls.
5. Darken the sky toward its upper edge and add restrained, broad light shafts.

Painterly textures, intricate scattered detail and soft volumetric shafts in the concepts may need simplified shaders, meshes or baked lighting to remain practical in the engine.
