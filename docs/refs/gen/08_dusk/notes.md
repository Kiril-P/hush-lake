## Diagnosis
- The pale, nearly uniform sky reads as daytime rather than dusk.
- The forest and mountains have little atmospheric separation from the camp.
- The hut glows, but stalls and paths lack warm pools of lantern light.
- The foreground ice is a flat blue plane with little variation or reflected light.
- The Eye is cropped at the right edge, leaving its pupil hard to read; the empty stalls add little story.

## Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Darken the sky and ice to teal dusk tones; add distance-based blue fog to separate forest and mountains.
2. Add amber lantern meshes and local light pools at stalls and paths, with restrained reflections on ice.
3. Strengthen the Eye's aqua emission and dark pupil at the existing edge; keep the ring distinct from nearby ice.
4. Add broad low-poly ice facets, subtle cracks and snow edging, with a few simple crates and bundled supplies by stalls.
5. Add a faint chimney particle plume and low ground fog for depth and life.

The storybook's painterly clouds, intricate reflections and detailed props are illustrative rather than literal primitive-geometry targets. All variants still crop the pupil at the frame edge; they do not solve its visibility fully.
