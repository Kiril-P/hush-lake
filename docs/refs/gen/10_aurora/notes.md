## Diagnosis
- The central beam blows out the Eye and obscures the stations behind it.
- The aurora is a uniform blurry stripe with little variation in shape or depth.
- Mountains and trees crowd together at similar contrast, flattening the horizon.
- The ice glow washes out its surface and competes with the hole's silhouette.
- Station surroundings lack small fishing props and localized warm light.

## Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Reduce beam opacity and bloom; give the Eye a dark pupil, aqua surround and thin amber ring.
2. Add blue distance fog between the forest and mountains, with darker foreground trees.
3. Shape the aurora with layered translucent teal ribbons and restrained vertical streaks.
4. Give the ice radial low-poly facets and a softer aqua falloff around the Eye.
5. Add small amber lanterns and simple crates, fishing racks and sleds beside stations.

The painterly aurora, intricate reflections and atmospheric shafts in the variants are aspirational; approximate them with simple shaders and translucent geometry rather than expecting exact real-time reproduction.
