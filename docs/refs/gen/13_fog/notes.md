## Diagnosis
- The uniform lavender sky gives little depth or sense of dusk.
- Fog flattens the forest into a single pale band instead of separating tree layers.
- The ice has conspicuous radial stripes and little material variation.
- The central hut lacks a clear warm light to anchor the distant composition.
- The foreground has no visible Eye or nearby fishing details to focus the view.

## Wins, ordered by impact
1. Add a readable foreground Eye: aqua emissive faceted disk, black pupil and thin amber ring, surrounded by simple broken ice meshes.
2. Darken the sky toward teal and layer blue fog by distance, keeping nearby trees darker than the horizon.
3. Give the hut an amber emissive window and a subtle elongated warm reflection on the ice.
4. Replace strong radial ice stripes with broad low-contrast facets, sparse cracks and snow patches.
5. Add a few simple fishing props and a lantern near the foreground, keeping their silhouettes clear.

The variants introduce a large foreground Eye absent from the screenshot; treat its placement as a concept rather than existing layout. Painted brushwork, intricate ice refraction, smoke and detailed reflections are artistic suggestions, not literal primitive-level targets.
