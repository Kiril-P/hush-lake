## Diagnosis

- The Eye's broad blown-out glow obscures the structure of its aqua iris.

3. Place small amber lantern lights on stalls and cast soft pools onto nearby snow and ice.
4. Add low-poly ice facets and subtle cracks, with aqua light falling off across the foreground.
5. Add simple fishing gear, crates and snow caps near the existing stalls.
