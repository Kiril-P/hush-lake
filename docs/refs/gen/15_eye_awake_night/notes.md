## Diagnosis
- The Eye's pupil is lost in the overexposed aqua beam.
- The beam forms a hard-edged column that obscures the far shore.
- Bright radial ice shards compete with the Eye for attention.
- The forest and mountains stack at similar contrast, flattening depth.
- The empty foreground ice lacks texture and small lived-in details.
## Wins
1. Darken the pupil and narrow the amber rim; reduce bloom around the Eye.
2. Fade the aqua beam at its edges and toward the sky, retaining the shore silhouette.
3. Add blue distance fog between forest and mountain layers.
4. Dim the shard emissive material and add subtle faceted ice reflections.
5. Place a few amber lanterns and simple fishing props near the existing structures.
Painted texture and complex volumetric light in concept art would need simplified shaders and lighting in-game.
