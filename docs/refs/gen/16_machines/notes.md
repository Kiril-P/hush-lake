## Diagnosis
- Foreground machine uprights obscure the Eye and dominate the center.
- Pale lavender sky and distant mountains lack the cold teal dusk palette.
- Forest and mountains have little fog separation, flattening the horizon.
- Ice is a broad uniform blue with sparse surface variation and harsh cyan shards.
- The lantern blooms intensely while machines and shoreline lack localized light and small props.

## Wins, ordered by impact
1. Keep a clear sightline to the Eye's aqua hole, black pupil, and thin amber ring between machine supports.
2. Darken and desaturate the sky and ice toward teal; add blue distance fog between forest and mountains.
3. Add subdued amber point lights at existing stations and a small warm pool around the foreground lantern.
4. Add sparse polygonal ice cracks, snow caps on machinery, and subtle cyan edge light on belts.
5. Add a few simple crates or a mug near the foreground station for a lived-in scale cue.

The painted clouds, detailed reflections, animals, and elaborate surface textures in the storybook variant exceed a primitive-based real-time treatment. The variants also invent or alter sign text and depict the Eye on the machine; retain the actual signs and place the Eye in the ice.
