## Diagnosis
- The Eye's bright concentric effects and crossing arms compete with its pupil.
- White hex borders dominate the ice and flatten its surface.
- The pale ice has little variation in depth or material.
- Machine silhouettes blend into their circular pads from overhead.
- The hut's warm light is isolated and the surrounding field lacks visual hierarchy.

## Wins, in order
1. Reduce Eye bloom and separate the dark pupil from the radial arms with a darker inner surround.
2. Tint hex seams blue and vary ice facets subtly to give the field depth without a bright grid.
3. Give each machine a dark, chunky top silhouette and a small amber indicator for readability.
4. Add restrained amber pools around the hut and owned machines, with soft teal ambient shadows.
5. Add sparse snow drifts and small practical props around the hut to suggest activity.

Painterly texture and elaborate atmospheric beams are concept treatments; approximate them with simple shaders and lights rather than literal geometry.
