# Diagnosis
- The pale, nearly uniform sky flattens the scene and weakens the dusk mood.
- Distant trees and mountains have little atmospheric separation from the near shore.
- The Eye's overexposed white glow obscures its aqua ring and dark pupil.
- The foreground ice is a broad, flat blue surface with few readable facets or cracks.
- Conveyor supports and shoreline work areas lack warm local light and small working props.

# Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Reduce Eye bloom and exposure; give its dark pupil a clean silhouette and a faceted aqua ring.
2. Darken and grade the sky and ice toward deep teal; add blue distance fog between tree and mountain layers.
3. Place small amber lanterns along belt junctions and near the hut, with restrained pools of light on nearby surfaces.
4. Add low-poly ice cracks and broad reflective patches, keeping them subordinate to the Eye.
5. Add simple crates, stacked logs and a fish rack near the hut to suggest work and scale.

The painted clouds, intricate reflections and volumetric shafts in the variants are aspirational; approximate them with simple gradients, decals and translucent meshes. Some variants add shoreline foreground and shift props beyond the exact reference layout.
