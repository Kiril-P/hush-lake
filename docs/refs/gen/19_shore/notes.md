## Diagnosis
- The pale, nearly uniform sky leaves the mountain skyline with little contrast.
- Repeated, evenly spaced tree cones form a flat band rather than a layered shore.
- White mountain peaks overlap the sky without enough atmospheric separation.
- Broad radial ice stripes dominate the foreground and flatten the lake surface.
- The distant Eye is obscured by competing beams and small, busy props.

## Wins (ordered by impact)
1. Darken the sky to teal and add blue distance fog between layered mountain and tree silhouettes.
2. Vary conifer heights and spacing, and add low faceted snowbanks at the shoreline.
3. Strengthen the Eye's aqua rim and black pupil at lake level, with a restrained glow and reflection.
4. Replace dominant ice stripes with sparse flat-shaded cracks and subtle, broken reflections of amber lights.
5. Add a few amber lanterns and snow caps to existing huts to establish scale and life.

The variants turn the Eye into a floating monument and retain a large marker beam; keep the Eye as a hole and omit the marker. Painted cloud detail and intricate reflections are artistic suggestions, not literal primitive-geometry requirements.
