## Diagnosis
- The pale, nearly uniform sky gives the low sun little presence.
- The forest and mountains merge into a similarly bright horizon, flattening depth.
- Broad ice stripes dominate the lake without convincing directional sunlight.
- The Eye is obscured by large cyan spikes and the foreground rail, so its pupil is hard to read.
- Scattered kiosks and the empty lake lack small fishing props that connect them to the setting.

## Wins, ordered by impact
1. Lower the directional sun and cast long soft-edged blue shadows across the ice; add a restrained amber horizon band.
2. Reduce the Eye's spiky geometry and bloom, and expose a black pupil inside its aqua ring.
3. Add blue distance fog between the lake, forest and mountains, with a subtle light shaft toward the horizon.
4. Replace harsh ice stripes with broad low-contrast facets, sparse cracks and a soft sun reflection.
5. Place a few low-poly crates, lanterns and fishing tools beside existing structures; use amber only for lights and interactive accents.

Painterly textures and volumetric light in the concepts are illustrative; approximate them with simple gradients, fog and restrained bloom in the engine.
